# Prototype Usability & Time-Efficiency Test Protocol

> **Status**: Template only. All fields below are blank and must be populated by the human researcher during user testing sessions.

## 1. Study Objective & Hypothesis

- **Objective**: Quantify whether HireLens grounded evidence and blind review interface reduces time-to-decision and increases screening accuracy compared to manual unstructured resume reading.
- **Primary Metric**: Time taken (in seconds) to evaluate a candidate against a multi-requirement job description.
- **Secondary Metric**: Accuracy in spotting ungrounded or exaggerated claims.
